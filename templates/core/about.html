Navalsingh Sahakari Shakkar Karkhana Maryadit

Navalsingh Sahakari Shakkar Karkhana Maryadit is a sugar manufacturing cooperative established in 1981 by Thakur Shivkumar Singh. Located in Navalnagar (Jhiri), Burhanpur, Madhya Pradesh, the factory has been serving the farming community for over four decades.

The factory provides an efficient cane booking and delivery system for local farmers, ensuring fair and transparent operations across all stages of sugar production — from cane reception to crystallised sugar.
